# Delivered Not Received

Fictional Northstar Retail. Policy delivered-not-received-v1. Region US. Effective 2026-01-01 until 2026-09-15 (end exclusive). Applies by event date.

## Policy

If an order shows as delivered but you have not received it, first check safe places around the address and ask household members and neighbours.

## Conditions and exceptions

If it is still missing, report it within 7 calendar days of the delivered date and support will escalate it. A delivered status on its own is not treated as proof that you received the parcel.

## What to expect

The report is handled as a support ticket, created after you confirm. No refund or replacement is promised before the review is complete.
