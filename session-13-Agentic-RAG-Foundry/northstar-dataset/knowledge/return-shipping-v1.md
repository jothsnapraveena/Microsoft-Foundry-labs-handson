# Return Shipping

Fictional Northstar Retail. Policy return-shipping-v1. Region US. Effective 2026-01-01 until 2026-09-15 (end exclusive). Applies by order date.

## Policy

Return shipping is free for returns approved under the defective-items policy.

## Conditions and exceptions

An eligible change-of-mind return carries a return shipping fee of $7.00 (700 cents) per return. The fee is shown before you confirm the return.

## What to expect

The fee is charged separately and is not deducted from the refund of the item price. No fee is charged for a request that is ineligible or sent for review.
